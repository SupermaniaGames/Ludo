        🤝 Ludo: Secret Ally

        4-player Ludo with secret pacts

        • 🤝 Ask for an ally: before you roll, tap 🤝 Ask for ally (or a player's name). Choose how the pact works: ⏳ Temporary (counts down, both must agree to renew) or ♾ Continuous. The other player must accept, or the request runs out.
        • 🏁 Victory: Duo means you both bring all tokens home to win together. Solo means you each win on your own. Allies never cut each other, anyone else still can.
        • 🛡 Shield: once per game, protect one token from being cut during the next player's turn. Raise it on your own turn.
        • ⚠ Danger: a red mark shows your token can be cut by one roll.
        • 🎁 Gift a roll to your ally, who must use it. 💔 Break & Cut after your pact has cut an opponent.
        • 😊 Quick reactions: 🤝 😂 😈 🛡️ 👏.

        Computer mode hides the other pacts from you. In Pass N Play everyone shares one screen, so look away!

        ⚔️ Ludo Duel

        A fast 2-player Ludo with 3 tokens each

        • ✂ Split steps: divide your roll between two tokens, so you almost never lose a turn.
        • 6️⃣ A 6 enters a token or moves 6, and gives a bonus roll. Three 6s are fine.
        • ⚔ Capture sends a token home and gives your token +1 step on every move after.
        • ⚡ Boost tile: push 1 extra step. 🛑 Shield tile: safe for one full turn. ★ is always safe.
        • 🏁 First to bring all 3 tokens home wins. Exact count needed to finish.
